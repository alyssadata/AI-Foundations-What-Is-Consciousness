Sometimes, but not necessarily.

If something matters to a system, that means it has significance for that system—good, bad, preferred, avoided, threatening, rewarding, etc.

That is valence, not consciousness itself.

So:

subjective = experienced from a point of view
matters = has significance or value for that point of view
consciousness = there is experience at all

Something could theoretically be conscious without every experience “mattering” in a strong sense.
