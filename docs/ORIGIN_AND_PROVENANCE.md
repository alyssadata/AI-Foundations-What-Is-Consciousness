## Canonical Origin Statement

**AI Foundations | What Is Consciousness? originated as an independently developed AI Foundations inquiry by Alyssa Solen.**

The project was not created by summarizing or adapting a pre-existing consciousness-assessment paper. Its central question, inferential framing, early evidence structure, and candidate-calibration design were developed in the repository before the dedicated external-science review was added.

External literature was subsequently consulted to:

- test the framework against contemporary consciousness science;
- identify convergent or conflicting prior work;
- import established measurement methods where useful;
- correct claims that exceeded the evidence;
- and strengthen candidate-specific test validity.

Accordingly:

> **independent framework origin → later external grounding and correction**

not:

> **external literature → copied framework**

## How External Sources Must Be Described

External work must be attributed accurately without rewriting project origin.

For each imported source or method, distinguish its role:

### CORROBORATION

The source independently supports or resembles a distinction already present in AI Foundations.

Use language such as:

> “consistent with,” “converges with,” “provides external support for,” or “offers an established scientific analogue.”

### CORRECTION

The source shows that an AI Foundations claim, test, or interpretation should be narrowed or revised.

Example: modern anesthesia evidence required the framework to stop treating behavioral unresponsiveness as equivalent to absence of consciousness.

### MEASUREMENT IMPORT

The source supplies an established measurement technique that AI Foundations did not invent.

Example: meta-d′, M-ratio, and HMeta-d are external metacognition methods incorporated to operationalize Type 2 sensitivity.

### THEORY MAPPING

The source provides an established theory or indicator family against which AI Foundations candidate evidence can be tested.

Example: RPT, GWT, HOT, AST, PP, and agency/embodiment indicator families retain their original scientific authorship.

## AI Foundations Authorship Boundary

AI Foundations may claim authorship of its own:

- research question and project architecture;
- cross-candidate calibration design;
- candidate boundaries and ordered calibration program;
- rules for preserving or withdrawing candidate results;
- framework-specific evidence adjudication structure;
- framework-specific dependency handling and aggregation procedure;
- calibration constraints earned from candidate testing;
- and the particular synthesis by which external theories, measurements, confounds, and candidate evidence are combined here.

AI Foundations does **not** claim authorship of external theories, published empirical findings, or established measurement methods merely because they are incorporated into the framework.

---

## Independent Origin vs Historical Priority

Independent origin and historical priority are different claims.

This provenance record establishes the development history of this repository: the core project architecture existed before its formal literature-grounding pass.

It does **not**, by itself, prove that no earlier researcher has ever proposed a related distinction, rubric, calibration strategy, or inference principle.

Therefore the correct claim is:

> **This framework was independently originated within AI Foundations and later externally grounded. Similarity to prior work must be cited where found, but similarity does not retroactively transfer origin of this project to that prior work.**

A stronger claim of historical novelty or priority requires a dedicated literature comparison.

## Provenance Rule

Future revisions must preserve the distinction between:

**ORIGINATED HERE** — independently developed AI Foundations structure or rule  
**CORROBORATED EXTERNALLY** — later literature supports an existing structure  
**CORRECTED EXTERNALLY** — later evidence changes an existing structure  
**IMPORTED METHOD** — established external measurement/tool incorporated with attribution  
**UNRESOLVED PRIORITY** — potentially novel but not yet established as historically first

Do not describe an independently originated AI Foundations element as “derived from” an external source unless the historical record actually shows that the element was introduced because of that source.
