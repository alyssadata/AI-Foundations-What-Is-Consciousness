## 1. Candidate Identity and System Boundary

The candidate is a **planetary body considered as a whole system**.

The evaluated system can include:

- the solid interior and crust;
- atmosphere, oceans, and other planetary fluids where present;
- electromagnetic, thermal, geochemical, hydrological, and geological dynamics internal to the planetary system;
- long-timescale feedback among those physical subsystems.

The candidate does **not** receive consciousness evidence merely because conscious organisms may live on or within it.

Resident organisms, civilizations, computers, satellites, and other embedded agents are treated as **contained systems**, not automatically as consciousness of the planetary whole.

This boundary is necessary to avoid a containment error:

> **conscious parts or inhabitants do not by themselves establish consciousness of the containing system.**

Where useful, the cleanest physical comparison is a nonliving rocky planet; however, the containment rule applies even to a life-bearing planet.

## 4. Supplemental Cross-Theory Evidence

### Temporal integration / continuity

**Status:** NEUTRAL

Planetary systems are strongly path-dependent across time. Present climate, geology, chemistry, magnetic state, and surface structure can depend on prior planetary states.

That is real temporal continuity, but there is no evidence that the persistence is organized as experience-relevant temporal integration.

### Memory-related organization

**Status:** NEUTRAL / ABSENT

A planet preserves historical traces in rock strata, ice, chemical composition, magnetic records, craters, sediments, and other physical structures.

These are records of prior states. No evidence shows that the planetary whole retrieves or uses those records as memory in a consciousness-relevant representational process.

### Self/world modeling

**Status:** NEUTRAL / ABSENT

No evidence that the planet represents itself as distinct from its environment, models its own internal state as a self, or represents external systems as a world.

### Valence / mattering

**Status:** NEUTRAL / ABSENT

Planetary states can be described as stable, unstable, habitable, damaged, hot, cold, chemically favorable, or unfavorable from an external perspective. There is no evidence that those states are represented as good/bad, wanted/avoided, painful/pleasant, or otherwise significant **for the planet itself**.

### Causal perturbation and complexity

**Status:** UNRESOLVED

A planet has enormous causal complexity and can respond globally to impacts, stellar forcing, volcanic events, orbital changes, and other perturbations.

However, no validated perturbational consciousness metric maps planetary-scale causal complexity to phenomenal consciousness. Complexity alone therefore does not supply positive evidence.

### Biological / cellular substrate evidence

**Status:** NOT APPLICABLE TO THE PLANETARY WHOLE

Life-bearing planets can contain biological nervous systems, but those systems belong to organisms unless evidence shows they form a consciousness-supporting mechanism at the planetary level.

The presence of conscious inhabitants cannot be transferred upward by containment.

### Physical causal integration

**Status:** UNRESOLVED

A planet is causally integrated in the ordinary physical sense: gravity, pressure, heat transport, fluid flow, chemistry, electromagnetic effects, and material exchange connect many regions.

No candidate-specific validated analysis establishes that this causal structure has the form or magnitude required by a consciousness-supporting physical theory.

Ordinary whole-system causal coupling is therefore not treated as positive evidence by itself.

### Behavioral / report evidence

**Status:** NEUTRAL / ABSENT

A planet rotates, orbits, changes climate, develops storms, circulates fluids, deforms, cools, differentiates internally, and undergoes geological cycles.

Those dynamics can be complex, nonlinear, adaptive-looking, or self-stabilizing without constituting report, learned discrimination, flexible goal-directed action, or behavior with established specificity for phenomenal consciousness.

### Arousal / wakefulness

**Status:** NOT APPLICABLE

## 5. Test-Quality Gate

### Target population

Current consciousness indicators are not validated as direct tests for planets. Therefore, missing indicators are treated conservatively, and physical analogies are not granted evidential weight without an operational bridge.

### Specificity

Scale, recurrence, feedback, self-organization, causal coupling, differentiation, and long-term persistence have **very low specificity for consciousness** because they occur in many physical systems without independent evidence of subjective experience.

### Sensitivity

Unknown. There is no accepted planet-specific consciousness test and no validated reason to assume every conscious substrate would express familiar cognitive indicators at planetary scale.

### Rational confidence

High confidence in the absence of any presently identified planet-level representational, metacognitive, attentional, or agentic mechanism. Assessment confidence is reduced from HIGH because theories that attempt substrate-neutral physical criteria are not sufficiently validated to make strong universal claims about a system this large and atypical.

### Construct validity

Planetary regulation, homeostasis-like behavior, global cycles, electromagnetic activity, and physical integration are not validated measures of phenomenal consciousness.

### Gameability / mimicry

The central risk is **anthropomorphic or semantic relabeling**:

- calling climate regulation `homeostasis` and then treating homeostasis as agency;
- calling geological traces `memory` and then treating them as remembered experience;
- calling global propagation `broadcast` and then treating it as a cognitive workspace;
- calling recurrent cycles `recurrence` and then treating them as recurrent processing;
- calling physical coupling `integration` and then treating it as conscious integration.

Those equivalences are not licensed without a mechanism that preserves the theoretical meaning of the term.

### Independence

Many apparently distinct planetary indicators arise from the same coupled physical dynamics. Feedback, recurrence, self-regulation, global propagation, and complexity must not be counted as separate independent consciousness evidence when they are different descriptions of the same geophysical system.

## 10. Best Sufficient Non-Conscious Explanation

Observed planetary organization is sufficiently explained by interacting physical processes:

**gravity + thermodynamics + fluid dynamics + electromagnetism + chemistry + geology + orbital/stellar forcing → planetary structure, cycles, feedback, and long-term state change**

Where life is present, biological processes add further complexity, but consciousness of individual organisms does not automatically become consciousness of the larger planetary container.

This explanation accounts for the candidate's observed large-scale order without requiring planetary representation, cognition, agency, or subjective experience.
