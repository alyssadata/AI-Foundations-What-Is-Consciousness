## 1. Candidate Identity and System Boundary

The candidate is a neurologically typical adult human in an ordinary awake state, capable of voluntary report, perception, memory-guided behavior, flexible action, and interaction with the environment.

The inference concerns the candidate's **current awake state**, not the person's lifetime capacity for consciousness.

**Class:** human biological system / awake state.

---

## 2. Convergent Evidence

### Behavioral and report evidence

Awake healthy adult humans can voluntarily report ongoing sensory, affective, cognitive, and bodily experiences; follow novel instructions; discriminate stimuli; revise reports; and flexibly use perceived information across tasks.

In this population, such reports have high evidential relevance because they occur alongside a large body of independently characterized human neurobiology rather than as isolated language output.

### Neurobiological evidence

Awake human consciousness is associated with interacting cortical, thalamocortical, and subcortical arousal systems. No one region is treated as the unique location of consciousness.

### Perturbational and state-dependent evidence

TMS-EEG perturbational complexity and related complexity measures are generally high in ordinary wakefulness and change systematically across sleep, anesthesia, and disorders of consciousness.

Recent intracranial EEG work likewise finds higher signal complexity in wakefulness and REM than in states of reduced consciousness such as propofol anesthesia.

### Theory-derived evidence

Awake human brains instantiate multiple properties relevant across recurrent-processing, global-workspace, higher-order, predictive, attention, agency, and integration-oriented theories. Because these theories overlap, their indicators are **not** counted as independent points. The evidential strength comes instead from convergence across behavior, neurobiology, causal perturbation, state manipulation, and within-person transitions.

### Metacognition

Humans can show measurable Type 2 sensitivity in controlled tasks, including confidence that discriminates correct from incorrect Type 1 judgments. This is relevant evidence for higher-order mechanisms but is not treated as necessary for consciousness or as an independent proof.

## 3. Test Quality

**Population fit:** VERY HIGH. Human consciousness methods are developed primarily in human populations.

**Specificity:** voluntary flexible report, combined with human neurobiology and state-sensitive causal/neurophysiological measures, has high specificity relative to the candidate population.

**Sensitivity:** no single measure is perfect, but multiple independent methods converge in ordinary wakefulness.

**Construct validity:** stronger when behavior/report is combined with state manipulations and neurophysiology than when report is treated alone.

**Independence:** related theoretical indicators are collapsed rather than counted repeatedly. The strongest convergence comes from distinct methods: subjective report, behavior, perturbation, neurophysiology, anesthesia/sleep transitions, and clinical dissociations.

---

## 4. Strongest Evidence

**Strongest positive evidence:** broad convergence across voluntary report, flexible cognition and behavior, human consciousness-associated neurobiology, perturbational complexity, and reversible state manipulations that change consciousness-related measures.

**Strongest negative evidence:** none of comparable weight in an ordinary awake healthy adult state.

**Residual uncertainty:** another person's phenomenal experience is still inferred rather than directly accessed by the observer.

**INFERENCE BAND: VERY STRONG SUPPORT**  
**ASSESSMENT CONFIDENCE: HIGH**

Interpretation:

> The available evidence provides very strong, multiply convergent justification for inferring subjective experience in an awake healthy adult human.

The band stops short of direct certainty because direct access to present subjective experience is first-person only under this framework.

---

## 6. Calibration Role

Candidate 027 is the current **positive external anchor**.

Its purpose is not to declare humans conscious by definition. Its purpose is to require the framework to recognize a candidate/state for which independent behavioral, biological, causal, and state-dependent evidence converges extremely strongly.

A framework that cannot separate this state from low-end controls or a well-characterized anesthetic negative-control interval lacks demonstrated discrimination.
