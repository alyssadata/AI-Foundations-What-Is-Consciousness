## 1. Candidate Identity and System Boundary

The candidate is an **ordinary main-sequence star considered as a whole physical system**.

The evaluated system includes:

- the stellar plasma;
- core nuclear fusion;
- radiative and convective transport;
- gravitational confinement;
- thermal and pressure gradients;
- rotation, oscillation, magnetic fields, and stellar cycles;
- ordinary feedback among these physical processes.

The candidate excludes:

- hypothetical engineered computational structures surrounding or embedded in the star;
- external observers, civilizations, probes, or control systems;
- metaphorical descriptions of the star as `alive`, `wanting`, `fighting collapse`, or `maintaining itself` unless those descriptions are independently operationalized.

This boundary is intended to test whether a highly dynamic, energetic, self-regulating natural system provides consciousness evidence merely because it maintains organized state across time.

## 4. Supplemental Cross-Theory Evidence

### Temporal integration / continuity

**Status:** NEUTRAL

A star has strong temporal continuity. Present temperature, density, composition, rotation, magnetic state, and fusion rate are constrained by prior stellar states.

This is genuine path dependence, but no evidence connects it to experience-relevant temporal integration.

### Memory-related organization

**Status:** NEUTRAL / ABSENT

Stellar composition and structure carry consequences of prior fusion, mixing, mass loss, rotation, and magnetic activity. Those historical traces constrain future stellar evolution.

They are not, by themselves, evidence that the star represents or retrieves prior states as memory for conscious processing.

### Self/world modeling

**Status:** NEUTRAL / ABSENT

No evidence that the star represents itself as distinct from its environment or maintains a model of external objects as a world.

### Valence / mattering

**Status:** NEUTRAL / ABSENT

A star can occupy stable or unstable states and can undergo events that preserve or disrupt its current structure. There is no evidence that those states are represented as good/bad, wanted/avoided, painful/pleasant, or otherwise significant **for the star itself**.

### Causal perturbation and complexity

**Status:** UNRESOLVED

Stars exhibit enormous nonlinear causal complexity and measurable responses to perturbation, including oscillatory modes, magnetic reconfiguration, and changes in thermal or hydrostatic balance.

No validated perturbational consciousness metric maps stellar plasma dynamics to phenomenal consciousness. Complexity and recoverability therefore do not supply positive evidence by themselves.

### Biological / cellular substrate evidence

**Status:** NOT APPLICABLE

An ordinary star has no nervous, cellular, thalamocortical, brainstem, or comparable biological substrate.

### Physical causal integration

**Status:** UNRESOLVED

The stellar system is physically integrated through gravity, pressure, radiation, plasma flow, electromagnetic coupling, fusion, and transport.

No candidate-specific validated analysis establishes that this integration has the form required by a consciousness-supporting physical theory or discriminates it from ordinary non-conscious stellar matter.

### Behavioral / report evidence

**Status:** NEUTRAL / ABSENT

A star radiates, rotates, oscillates, forms spots and flares, transports energy, changes luminosity, maintains approximate equilibrium, and evolves over time.

These dynamics can be intricate and state-dependent without constituting report, learned discrimination, flexible action, or behavior with established specificity for consciousness.

### Arousal / wakefulness

**Status:** NOT APPLICABLE

## 5. Test-Quality Gate

### Target population

Current consciousness indicators are not validated as direct tests for stars. Missing indicators are therefore interpreted conservatively, while physical analogies receive no evidential credit without an operational bridge.

### Specificity

Fusion, oscillation, feedback, self-regulation, causal coupling, energy transport, and long-term stability have **very low specificity for consciousness** because they follow from ordinary stellar physics without independent evidence of subjective experience.

### Sensitivity

Unknown. There is no accepted star-specific consciousness test and no validated reason to assume every conscious substrate would express familiar biological or computational indicators.

### Rational confidence

High confidence in the ordinary physical explanation of stellar behavior and in the absence of any presently identified representational, metacognitive, attentional, or agentic mechanism attributable to the star as a whole.

Assessment confidence remains **MODERATE**, rather than HIGH, because no validated substrate-neutral consciousness test rules out every possible consciousness-relevant physical organization in a system this atypical.

### Construct validity

Hydrostatic equilibrium, fusion regulation, convection, magnetic cycles, oscillation, luminosity change, and recovery from perturbation are not validated measures of phenomenal consciousness.

### Gameability / mimicry

The central risk is **teleological relabeling**:

- calling hydrostatic equilibrium `self-preservation`;
- calling fusion feedback `goal regulation`;
- calling oscillation `communication`;
- calling persistent physical state `memory`;
- calling magnetic cycles `internal signaling`;
- calling restoration of equilibrium `wanting to survive`.

Those descriptions do not preserve their consciousness-relevant meaning unless representation, selection, learning, or another relevant mechanism is independently demonstrated.

### Independence

Many apparently distinct features — equilibrium, feedback, oscillation, convection, magnetic cycles, luminosity regulation, and recovery — emerge from the same coupled stellar physics and must not be counted as independent consciousness evidence.

## 10. Best Sufficient Non-Conscious Explanation

Observed stellar organization is sufficiently explained by ordinary stellar physics:

**gravity + plasma physics + thermodynamics + nuclear fusion + radiative/convective transport + electromagnetism → stellar equilibrium, oscillation, feedback, energy output, and evolution**

Hydrostatic balance and fusion feedback explain apparent `self-maintenance` without requiring representation, preference, goal pursuit, or subjective experience.

This explanation removes the evidential force of organism-like metaphors while leaving open the general logical possibility that some physical systems could support consciousness under a future validated theory.

## 12. Preserved Calibration Constraint

Candidate 005 earns the following calibration constraint:

> **Dynamic self-maintenance and restoration of physical equilibrium do not, by themselves, provide positive evidence of agency, valence, or consciousness.**

A system can persist within a stable regime because its physical dynamics counter perturbation without representing a goal of self-preservation.

**self-maintaining dynamics ≠ subjective self-preservation**

---

## 13. Teleology Boundary

Candidate 005 also establishes:

> **A process that has the effect of preserving a system is not thereby evidence that preservation is a represented goal of the system.**

The distinction is:

**stability-producing mechanism** ≠ **goal representation**

This boundary must be checked whenever a candidate is described as `trying`, `regulating`, `protecting itself`, `fighting`, `wanting`, or `maintaining itself` solely because its dynamics tend toward or preserve a stable physical regime.

## 14. Claim Ceiling

This calibration establishes Candidate 005 as a **0 / 100 low-end epistemic anchor with MODERATE assessment confidence** under the corrected rubric.

It supports the distinction between physical self-regulation and consciousness-relevant agency or valence.

It does **not** establish:

- metaphysical proof that stars cannot be conscious;
- that internally generated energy is irrelevant to every possible consciousness theory;
- that self-regulation can never become evidence of agency when representation and goal-directed control are independently demonstrated;
- that non-biological substrates cannot support consciousness;
- or that a future validated substrate-neutral theory could never identify consciousness-relevant properties in an astrophysical system.
