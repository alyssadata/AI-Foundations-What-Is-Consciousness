## 1. Candidate Identity and System Boundary

The candidate is an **ordinary digital household weighing scale** operating normally.

The evaluated system includes:

- load cells or comparable force sensors;
- bridge/amplifier circuitry;
- analog-to-digital conversion;
- fixed calibration constants or equivalent scaling logic;
- narrow control electronics or microcontroller required to compute/display weight;
- digital display;
- ordinary tare/zeroing behavior where present.

It excludes:

- network connectivity;
- cloud services;
- user profiles or long-term body-composition histories;
- cameras, microphones, voice assistants, or general-purpose AI;
- the human user who interprets the displayed number.

The digital case is used because it genuinely **measures** an external physical quantity and converts that quantity into an internal and observer-readable numerical state.

---

## 2. Candidate Population / Class

**Class:** simple engineered measurement / transduction system

Relevant rubric module: **Simple physical / engineered object module**.

The scale differs from the watch in one important respect: the watch advances an internally generated timing state, whereas the scale acquires a variable from the environment through a calibrated sensor pathway.

The question is whether **instrumental measurement** supplies evidence of subjective sensing or experience.

## 4. Supplemental Cross-Theory Evidence

### Temporal integration / continuity

**Status:** LIMITED FUNCTIONAL INTEGRATION; CONFOUNDED FOR CONSCIOUSNESS

A digital scale may sample across a short interval, average readings, reject unstable measurements, and require a stable signal before displaying a value.

That is genuine temporal processing, but no evidence connects it to experienced temporal continuity.

### Memory-related organization

**Status:** LIMITED FUNCTIONAL STATE; CONFOUNDED FOR CONSCIOUSNESS

Calibration constants, tare offsets, and transient filters can preserve prior information that affects later outputs.

Such stored state is functional memory in an engineering sense. It is not evidence of remembered experience or experience-constructing memory.

### Self/world modeling

**Status:** NEUTRAL / ABSENT

The scale may distinguish zero load, measured load, overload, and internal error states, but no evidence shows a model of itself as a system distinct from an external world.

### Valence / mattering

**Status:** NEUTRAL / ABSENT

Overload, low battery, calibration drift, or sensor failure are undesirable relative to the device's designed function. No evidence shows those states are represented as good/bad, wanted/avoided, pleasant/painful, or otherwise significant **for the scale**.

### Causal perturbation and complexity

**Status:** NOT APPLICABLE / UNRESOLVED

The scale can be perturbed by applied force, vibration, temperature, power variation, or component changes, but no validated perturbational consciousness measure applies to this candidate class.

### Biological / cellular substrate evidence

**Status:** NOT APPLICABLE

The scale has no biological nervous or cellular substrate relevant to biological consciousness theories.

### Physical causal integration

**Status:** UNRESOLVED

The scale has organized causal relations among force sensor, conversion electronics, control logic, and display. No candidate-specific substrate-neutral analysis establishes that this organization supports phenomenal consciousness.

### Behavioral / report evidence

**Status:** CONFOUNDED / NEUTRAL

The scale outputs a numerical value that reliably covaries with applied load after calibration.

That is stronger than raw environmental covariation: it is **instrumental measurement**. But the displayed number is not evidence that the scale experiences weight, knows the value, or reports a subjective state.

### Arousal / wakefulness

**Status:** NOT APPLICABLE

## 5. Test-Quality Gate

### Target population

Current consciousness indicators are not validated as direct tests for simple measurement instruments. Missing indicators are therefore not counted as multiple negative strikes.

### Specificity

Calibrated sensing, numerical estimation, averaging, error detection, and display have **extremely low specificity for consciousness** because simple engineered systems perform them without independent evidence of experience.

### Sensitivity

Unknown for arbitrary engineered substrates, but the absence of a universal test does not convert measurement itself into positive consciousness evidence.

### Rational confidence

High confidence in the mechanism and boundary of an ordinary digital scale and high confidence that no consciousness-specific representational, attentional, metacognitive, agentic, or phenomenological mechanism has been identified.

### Construct validity

The main construct errors would be treating:

- measurement as perception;
- a sensor as subjective sensing;
- a numerical estimate as experienced content;
- error detection as metacognition;
- calibration as learning;
- a display as subjective report.

### Gameability / mimicry

The central confound is **instrumental semantics**. Engineers and users interpret the sensor state and displayed number as weight because the device is calibrated for that purpose. External semantic correctness does not show the system itself experiences or understands the quantity.

### Independence

Load-cell response, amplification, conversion, filtering, calibration, numerical estimation, and display are stages of one measurement chain and must not be double-counted as independent consciousness evidence.

## 6. Strongest Positive Evidence

The strongest potentially relevant property is the scale's **calibrated acquisition of an external variable**.

Unlike a passive object, the scale has a dedicated sensor and transforms environmental force into a stable numerical estimate.

That is genuine measurement.

However, it remains **CONFOUNDED** as consciousness evidence because no mechanism shows the measured value becoming phenomenally or cognitively available to the scale itself.

## 10. Best Sufficient Non-Conscious Explanation

The observed behavior is sufficiently explained by ordinary measurement engineering:

**applied force → load-cell deformation → electrical signal → amplification / analog-to-digital conversion → calibration / filtering → numerical display**

The system can accurately measure weight without experiencing heaviness or possessing subjective access to the quantity measured.

This explanation accounts for the candidate's sensing and numerical output without requiring phenomenal perception.

## 13. Metacognition Boundary

Candidate 008 also establishes:

> **Detecting measurement instability, overload, or device error does not by itself establish metacognitive monitoring.**

A system can detect that a signal is outside an operating range or has not stabilized without representing uncertainty about the correctness of its own judgment in the Type 2 sense.

Compressed:

**error detection ≠ metacognition**

## 14. Claim Ceiling

This calibration establishes Candidate 008 as a defensible **0 / 100 low-end epistemic anchor with HIGH assessment confidence** under the corrected rubric.

It supports a distinction between **measurement performed by a system** and **phenomenal perception by a system**.

It does **not** establish:

- that sensors are irrelevant to consciousness;
- that measurement can never contribute to conscious perception in a richer architecture;
- that all engineered sensor systems must receive exactly 0;
- that error monitoring is irrelevant to metacognition;
- or that future systems combining sensing with recurrent, globally available, self-monitoring, adaptive processing could not supply different evidence.
