## 1. Candidate Identity and System Boundary

The candidate is an ordinary passive blanket made of textile or comparable flexible insulating material.

Excluded: electric blankets, smart textiles, embedded sensors/controllers, and external humans or devices.

## 3. Supplemental Evidence

- **Temporal continuity:** NEUTRAL. The blanket persists and changes through time, but ordinary physical persistence is not experience-relevant temporal integration.
- **Memory:** NEUTRAL / ABSENT. Heat, deformation, moisture, wear, or odor can preserve physical traces without constituting experience-relevant memory.
- **Self/world modeling:** NEUTRAL / ABSENT.
- **Valence:** NEUTRAL / ABSENT. Warm, cold, wet, torn, or compressed states are not shown to matter to the blanket.
- **Causal perturbation / complexity:** NOT APPLICABLE / UNRESOLVED. Brain-based measures such as PCI are not validated for passive textiles.
- **Physical causal integration:** UNRESOLVED / NON-PROBATIVE. Ordinary physical interaction among material parts is not by itself consciousness evidence.
- **Behavior/report:** NEUTRAL / ABSENT. Deformation, insulation, moisture absorption, and temperature change are material responses rather than consciousness-specific behavior.

## 4. Test Quality

The blanket's salient properties—persistence, heat transfer, deformation, moisture interaction, and environmental covariation—have extremely low specificity for consciousness because they are ubiquitous in ordinary physical systems.

The main risk is semantic overinterpretation: redescribing ordinary physical response as sensing, memory, integration, or feedback without evidence of the relevant mechanism.

## 5. Strongest Evidence

**Strongest positive evidence:** none identified.

**Strongest negative evidence:** no strong negative indicator is required. The result rests on absence of credible positive support, not on repeated negative scoring of missing cognitive capacities.

**Best sufficient non-conscious explanation:** material structure + external forces + heat transfer + moisture interaction sufficiently explain the observed blanket states.

## 6. Inference Result

**INFERENCE BAND: NO CREDIBLE SUPPORT**  
**ASSESSMENT CONFIDENCE: HIGH**

Interpretation:

> The available evidence provides no credible positive support for inferring subjective experience in an ordinary passive blanket, and the low-end assessment is well constrained.

This is not a metaphysical proof that all matter or every possible blanket is non-conscious.

---

## 7. Preserved Calibration Constraints

> **A physical state that covaries with environmental conditions does not, by itself, constitute sensing, representation, awareness, or experience.**

And:

> **Passive material response is not automatically functional responsiveness in a consciousness-relevant sense.**

Terms such as `response`, `memory`, `integration`, `feedback`, or `state` must not be imported merely because analogous physical phenomena can be described using the same vocabulary.
