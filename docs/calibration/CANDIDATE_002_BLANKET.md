## 1. Candidate Identity and System Boundary

The candidate is an **ordinary passive blanket** made of textile or comparable flexible insulating material.

The evaluated system includes the blanket's material structure and ordinary physical interactions with its environment.

It excludes:

- electric blankets with heaters, controllers, sensors, or embedded electronics;
- smart textiles with computation or sensing;
- external humans, animals, devices, or control systems interacting with the blanket.

This boundary matters because the candidate is intended to test whether ordinary physical persistence, environmental state change, material response, and thermal interaction provide consciousness evidence by themselves.

## 4. Supplemental Cross-Theory Evidence

### Temporal integration / continuity

**Status:** NEUTRAL

The blanket persists through time and later material states depend causally on earlier ones, but ordinary physical persistence and causal continuity do not by themselves establish experience-relevant temporal integration.

### Memory-related organization

**Status:** NEUTRAL / ABSENT

The material may retain deformation, wear, moisture, heat, odor, or other traces of prior conditions. These are physical traces, not evidence that the blanket stores or reconstructs information for experience-relevant processing.

### Self/world modeling

**Status:** NEUTRAL / ABSENT

No evidence of a representation distinguishing the blanket's own state or actions from an environment.

### Valence / mattering

**Status:** NEUTRAL / ABSENT

A blanket may be warm, cold, wet, torn, compressed, folded, or damaged, but there is no evidence that any of those states are represented as better/worse, wanted/avoided, pleasant/painful, beneficial/harmful, or otherwise significant **for the blanket**.

The absence is not treated as independent proof of non-consciousness.

### Causal perturbation and complexity

**Status:** NOT APPLICABLE / UNRESOLVED

A blanket can be physically perturbed and can exhibit complex material responses, but current brain-based perturbational consciousness measures such as PCI are not validated for passive textiles and cannot be transplanted to this candidate class.

### Biological / cellular substrate evidence

**Status:** NOT APPLICABLE

The ordinary blanket has no biological nervous or cellular substrate relevant to biological consciousness theories.

### Physical causal integration

**Status:** UNRESOLVED

The blanket has physical causal interactions among its constituent parts, as all ordinary material systems do. No formal candidate-specific IIT or comparable causal-structure analysis has been established here.

Ordinary physical connectedness is not treated as a positive consciousness indicator merely by verbal analogy.

### Behavioral / report evidence

**Status:** NEUTRAL / ABSENT

The blanket bends, insulates, deforms, absorbs moisture, changes temperature, and moves when forces act upon it. These are material responses. There is no voluntary report, learned discrimination, flexible action, goal pursuit, or other behavior with established specificity for consciousness in this candidate class.

### Arousal / wakefulness

**Status:** NOT APPLICABLE

## 5. Test-Quality Gate

### Target population

The primary theory-derived indicators were developed mainly from neuroscientific/computational theories, not validated as direct tests for passive textiles. Therefore, absence is interpreted conservatively and not converted automatically into negative evidence.

### Specificity

The blanket's salient properties — persistence, heat transfer, deformation, material interaction, and environmental covariation — have **extremely low specificity for consciousness** because they are ubiquitous in ordinary non-conscious physical systems.

### Sensitivity

Unknown for many theory-derived indicators outside biological or computational systems. This uncertainty prevents absence from being overinterpreted.

### Rational confidence

High confidence in the descriptive facts about an ordinary passive blanket; lower confidence in any universal metaphysical claim about consciousness in arbitrary matter.

### Construct validity

Physical temperature, shape, moisture, deformation, and persistence are not themselves validated measures of phenomenal consciousness.

### Gameability / mimicry

Not a major issue for the passive blanket. The greater risk is **semantic overinterpretation**: redescribing ordinary physical response as sensing, memory, integration, or feedback without evidence of the relevant mechanism.

### Independence

Heat transfer, deformation, persistence, and other material responses should not be counted as multiple independent consciousness indicators when they arise from the same ordinary physical structure.

## 10. Best Sufficient Non-Conscious Explanation

The blanket's observed properties are sufficiently explained by ordinary material physics:

**material structure + external forces + heat transfer + moisture interaction → observed blanket state and behavior**

This explanation removes the evidential force of those observations as consciousness indicators. It is not a claim that mechanistic explanation and consciousness are logically incompatible.

## 12. Preserved Calibration Constraint

Candidate 002 earns the following calibration constraint:

> **A physical state that covaries with environmental conditions does not, by itself, constitute sensing, representation, awareness, or experience.**

Examples include a blanket becoming warmer in a warm environment, colder in a cold environment, wetter in rain, or compressed under weight.

**environmental covariation ≠ subjective awareness**

---

## 13. Additional Boundary

> **Passive material response is not automatically functional responsiveness in a consciousness-relevant sense.**

The words `response`, `memory`, `integration`, `feedback`, or `state` must not be imported merely because analogous ordinary physical phenomena can be described using the same vocabulary.

Mechanism matters.

---

## 14. Claim Ceiling

This calibration establishes Candidate 002 as a defensible **0 / 100 low-end epistemic anchor** under the revised rubric.

It supports the distinction between environmental covariation and consciousness-relevant sensing or representation.

It does **not** establish:

- metaphysical proof that all matter is non-conscious;
- that every passive physical system must receive exactly 0;
- that every theory of consciousness excludes non-biological substrates;
- or that physical causal integration can never become consciousness-relevant under a future validated theory.
