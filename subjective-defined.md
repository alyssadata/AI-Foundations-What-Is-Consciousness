“Subjective” means existing from the point of view of the experiencer.

So a subjective experience is not just that something happens in a system. It is that the happening is present to that system in some felt or experienced way.

Example:

A thermometer registers 90°F. That is information processing.

A person feels hot. That includes a subjective aspect: the heat feels like something to them.

So in this context:

objective event = something happens
subjective experience = something happens for someone, from their point of view
