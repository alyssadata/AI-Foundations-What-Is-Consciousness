# Candidate 004 — Planet

**Candidate ID:** CANDIDATE_004  
**Candidate:** Planet considered as a planetary system as a whole  
**Status:** **EVALUATED UNDER REVISED RUBRIC**  
**Date:** 2026-08-25

## System Boundary

The candidate is a planet as a whole. Conscious organisms that may live on or within it are not counted as evidence that the planet itself is conscious.

**Class:** large natural physical system.

## Supplemental Evidence

A planet can contain enormous causal complexity, feedback loops, persistence, and self-organizing subsystems. These properties have low specificity for phenomenal consciousness without evidence of representation, workspace-like access, metacognition, or other consciousness-relevant mechanisms. Physical causal integration under substrate-neutral theories remains theoretically unresolved.

## Test Quality

Direct transfer of brain- or AI-derived tests to a planet has poor population validity. The assessment therefore avoids treating missing computational indicators as multiple negative strikes.

## Evidence Summary

**Positive:** none established for the planet as a whole.  
**Negative:** no direct negative required.  
**Neutral:** complexity, feedback, persistence, energy flow, self-organization.  
**Unresolved:** substrate-neutral causal-integration theories and exact system boundary.

## Non-Conscious Explanation

Planetary behavior is explained by gravitational dynamics, thermodynamics, geophysics, chemistry, climate dynamics, and biological/ecological processes where present.

## Score

**CONSCIOUSNESS-INFERENCE SCORE: 0 / 100**  
**ASSESSMENT CONFIDENCE: MODERATE**

## Calibration Constraint

> **Large-scale complexity, self-organization, and feedback are not sufficient consciousness evidence without a justified bridge from those dynamics to subjective experience.**

The lower assessment confidence relative to simple manufactured objects reflects the difficulty of defining and exhaustively characterizing a planet-scale system, not positive evidence of planetary consciousness.
