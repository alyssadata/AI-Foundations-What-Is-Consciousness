# Candidate 006 — Flower

**Candidate ID:** CANDIDATE_006  
**Candidate:** Living flowering plant / flower-bearing plant without an animal nervous system  
**Status:** **EVALUATED UNDER REVISED RUBRIC**  
**Date:** 2026-08-25

## System Boundary and Class

The candidate is a living flowering plant, including its ordinary plant signaling and regulatory systems, but excluding animals, microbes, external computers, or human interpretation. **Class:** living non-neural biological organism.

## Primary Indicator Battery

Plants exhibit rich signaling, regulation, growth responses, chemical communication, circadian organization, and forms of physiological memory. Those facts justify distinguishing a flower from a blanket. However, the current evidence does not establish the theory-derived consciousness mechanisms in a way with strong specificity for phenomenal experience.

- **Recurrent/integrated processing:** UNRESOLVED / weakly suggestive at most. Distributed signaling and feedback exist, but a consciousness-relevant perceptual representational architecture is not established.
- **Global workspace:** NEUTRAL / ABSENT.
- **Higher-order / metacognition:** NEUTRAL / ABSENT.
- **Attention schema:** NEUTRAL / ABSENT.
- **Predictive processing:** UNRESOLVED. Adaptive anticipatory regulation is not automatically predictive coding in the consciousness-theory sense.
- **Agency / embodiment:** weak functional analogues exist through tropisms and adaptive growth, but they are low-specificity evidence and are treated as CONFOUNDED by non-conscious biological control explanations.

## Supplemental Evidence

Biological self-maintenance, environmental sensing, chemical/electrical signaling, and adaptive response are real. They distinguish the candidate from inert objects but do not currently provide strong evidence of subjective experience. No nervous system or accepted consciousness-supporting plant architecture has been demonstrated.

## Test Quality

This is an area where anthropomorphic language creates substantial false-positive risk. Terms such as "memory," "learning," "decision," and "communication" can describe plant physiology functionally without implying experience.

## Evidence Summary

**Strongest positive:** genuine biological sensing, integration, regulation, and adaptive history-dependence.  
**Status of that evidence:** weak / confounded as consciousness evidence because the same phenomena have established non-conscious physiological explanations.  
**Strongest negative:** no validated plant-specific consciousness marker or accepted architecture bridging these functions to subjective experience.  
**Unresolved:** substrate-neutral theories and disputed plant-consciousness proposals.

## Score

**CONSCIOUSNESS-INFERENCE SCORE: 2 / 100**  
**ASSESSMENT CONFIDENCE: MODERATE**

## Calibration Constraint

> **Biological sensing, signaling, adaptation, and memory-like state dependence can be real without thereby establishing phenomenal consciousness.**

The score is above the simple-object floor only because the living system exhibits genuine organized sensing and adaptive regulation; those features remain extremely weak and non-specific evidence for subjective experience.
