**Candidate ID:** CANDIDATE_014

**Status:** **EVALUATED UNDER REVISED RUBRIC**

## Boundary and Class

A contemporary vehicle with multiple ECUs, sensors, feedback controllers, diagnostics, and driver-assistance subsystems, but without an autonomous general agent controlling the vehicle. **Class:** complex distributed engineered control system.

## Indicator Summary

The car contains many specialized subsystems operating in parallel, numerous sensors, state persistence, feedback loops, and limited inter-module communication. Those features create superficial overlap with some theory-derived indicators. However, there is no established limited-capacity global workspace for flexible cognition, higher-order representation, attention schema, unified self/world model, learned general agency, or valence.

The strongest apparent positive—parallel specialized subsystems with shared vehicle-state information—is **CONFOUNDED / weak** because distributed control buses and coordinated engineering do not by themselves have high specificity for phenomenal consciousness.
