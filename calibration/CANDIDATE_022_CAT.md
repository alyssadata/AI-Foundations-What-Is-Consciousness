## Class and Evidence

**Class:** nonhuman mammal. Cats possess the mammalian neural systems, recurrent sensory processing, attention, learning, memory, sleep/wake organization, affective behavior, nociception and analgesia-responsive pain behavior, flexible goal-directed action, and integrated perception strongly associated with consciousness in mammals.

The 2024 New York Declaration states there is **strong scientific support** for attributions of conscious experience to other mammals.

Multiple independent biological, behavioral, causal, and comparative lines point positively. Language and human-style metacognition are not required. Remaining uncertainty is the ordinary other-minds limitation and incomplete theory of consciousness, not a lack of candidate-specific evidence.
