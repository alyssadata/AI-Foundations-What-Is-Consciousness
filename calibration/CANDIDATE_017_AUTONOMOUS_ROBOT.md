## Boundary and Class

The candidate is a non-LLM-specific robot that integrates sensor inputs, maintains internal state/memory, plans, and adapts actions to pursue goals. No claim is made that it implements any particular proprietary architecture. **Class:** embodied autonomous computational agent.

## Indicator Summary

**Positive / potentially relevant:** specialized sensory/control subsystems; multimodal integration; temporal persistence; learned or model-based action selection; minimal flexible agency; output-input contingency modeling; self/world distinction sufficient for navigation; and embodiment.

**Unresolved:** whether the architecture contains a true limited-capacity global workspace, broad broadcast, higher-order representations, attention schema, consciousness-relevant recurrent perceptual processing, or structured quality-space coding.

**Confounded:** verbal or behavioral displays of pain, preference, or selfhood unless they track independently inspectable internal mechanisms.
