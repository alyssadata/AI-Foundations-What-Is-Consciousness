# Candidate 020 — Insect

**Candidate ID:** CANDIDATE_020  
**Candidate:** Adult insect, with evidence interpreted across studied taxa such as bees, flies, and cockroaches  
**Status:** **EVALUATED UNDER REVISED RUBRIC**  
**Scientific checkpoint:** 2026-08-25

## Boundary and Class

Because "insect" spans enormous diversity, this record evaluates the taxonomic class cautiously rather than pretending all insects have identical evidence. **Class:** nonhuman animal.

## Evidence

The evidence base now includes centralized nervous systems, sensory integration, attention-like selection, associative and flexible learning, sleep-like states, prediction, self/other-related behaviors in some species, motivational trade-offs, and injury-related behavioral changes. A 2025 Royal Society review concluded that converging work increasingly raises the probability that insects may possess some form of subjective experience, while emphasizing that formal certainty is unavailable.

The 2024 New York Declaration on Animal Consciousness states that empirical evidence supports at least a **realistic possibility** of conscious experience in insects. Some pain-focused reviews find substantial or strong evidence in specific adult orders, although a 2026 critique of bee motivational-tradeoff evidence shows that individual paradigms remain contestable.

## Indicator Summary

- **Integrated/recurrent neural processing:** POSITIVE / biologically plausible.
- **Attention and selective processing:** POSITIVE in studied species.
- **Learning and flexible agency:** POSITIVE.
- **Prediction / memory:** POSITIVE in studied species.
- **Valence/pain evidence:** POSITIVE but taxon- and paradigm-dependent.
- **Self/world and metacognition:** limited / UNRESOLVED.
- **Homology to mammalian consciousness mechanisms:** lower than vertebrates, increasing theoretical uncertainty.

## Test Quality

Evidence is genuinely convergent but heterogeneous across species, and no single insect behavior is decisive. Generalizing from bees or flies to all insects lowers assessment confidence.

## Score

**CONSCIOUSNESS-INFERENCE SCORE: 60 / 100**  
**ASSESSMENT CONFIDENCE: MODERATE**

## Calibration Constraint

> **Small nervous systems and large phylogenetic distance from humans do not justify assigning zero consciousness evidence when multiple independent behavioral and neurobiological indicators converge.**
