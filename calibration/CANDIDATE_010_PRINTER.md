# Candidate 010 — Printer

**Candidate ID:** CANDIDATE_010  
**Candidate:** Ordinary office printer  
**Status:** **EVALUATED UNDER REVISED RUBRIC**

## Boundary and Class

A conventional printer including sensors, firmware, buffers, motors, error detection, and ordinary control logic, but excluding cloud AI services. **Class:** embedded engineered control system.

## Indicator Summary

The printer can sense paper state, retain job data, detect errors, coordinate subsystems, and execute conditional control. These are real functional capacities. They do not form credible evidence of a global workspace, higher-order self-monitoring in the consciousness-theory sense, attention schema, phenomenal perceptual integration, valence, or flexible learned agency.

Self-diagnostic messages are externally designed state reports and are therefore highly confounded as evidence of introspection.

## Score

**ASSESSMENT CONFIDENCE: HIGH**
