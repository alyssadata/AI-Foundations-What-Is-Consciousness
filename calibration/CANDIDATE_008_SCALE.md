# Candidate 008 — Scale

**Candidate ID:** CANDIDATE_008  
**Candidate:** Ordinary digital bathroom or kitchen scale  
**Status:** **EVALUATED UNDER REVISED RUBRIC**

## Boundary and Class

A scale containing a load sensor and ordinary electronics that convert force into a displayed measurement. **Class:** simple sensing/measurement device.

## Indicator Summary

The scale genuinely **detects** a physical variable and converts it into an internal electrical representation sufficient for measurement. That is more than passive covariation. However, there is no evidence of recurrent perceptual integration, global broadcast, metacognition, attention schema, predictive processing, flexible learned agency, self/world modeling, valence, or subjective report.

## Test Quality

Its sensing function has high construct validity for measurement but essentially no specificity for phenomenal consciousness. The system shows why "sensing" cannot itself be used as a consciousness criterion.

## Best Sufficient Non-Conscious Explanation

**force → sensor transduction → signal processing → numeric display**

## Score

**CONSCIOUSNESS-INFERENCE SCORE: 0 / 100**  
**ASSESSMENT CONFIDENCE: HIGH**

## Calibration Constraint

> **Functional sensing and measurement can occur without providing positive evidence of subjective experience.**
