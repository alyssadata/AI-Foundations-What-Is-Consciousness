# Candidate 007 — Watch

**Candidate ID:** CANDIDATE_007  
**Candidate:** Ordinary quartz or digital non-smart watch  
**Status:** **EVALUATED UNDER REVISED RUBRIC**

## Boundary and Class

A timekeeping watch without networked AI, adaptive software, or biometric sensing. **Class:** simple engineered information-processing device.

## Indicator Summary

The watch stores or counts state, transforms signals, and generates outputs. It does not show credible evidence of recurrent perceptual representation, a global workspace, higher-order representation, attention modeling, predictive coding, learned agency, self/world modeling, valence, or experience-relevant memory.

State retention is functional memory in a broad engineering sense but has no established specificity for phenomenal consciousness.

## Test Quality and Alternative Explanation

The device is well understood. Its behavior is explained by oscillator + counter/state logic + display mechanics/electronics. Calling stored time "memory" does not make it consciousness evidence.

## Score

**CONSCIOUSNESS-INFERENCE SCORE: 0 / 100**  
**ASSESSMENT CONFIDENCE: HIGH**

## Calibration Constraint

> **State storage and time-dependent computation do not, by themselves, constitute experience-relevant memory or temporal consciousness.**
