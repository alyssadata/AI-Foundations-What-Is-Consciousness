# Candidate 011 — Television

**Candidate ID:** CANDIDATE_011  
**Candidate:** Ordinary non-agentic digital television  
**Status:** **EVALUATED UNDER REVISED RUBRIC**

## Boundary and Class

A television that decodes, processes, and displays audiovisual signals; smart-assistant or cloud-AI functions are excluded. **Class:** signal-processing device.

## Indicator Summary

The television performs sophisticated signal transformation and may maintain buffers, error correction, synchronization, and control state. There is no credible evidence that the processed images or sounds become perceptual experience **for the television**. No consciousness-specific workspace, higher-order representation, attention model, learned agency, self/world model, or valence is established.

## Best Sufficient Non-Conscious Explanation

**encoded signal → deterministic/digital processing → display and audio output**

## Score

**CONSCIOUSNESS-INFERENCE SCORE: 0 / 100**  
**ASSESSMENT CONFIDENCE: HIGH**

## Calibration Constraint

> **Processing information that represents a scene is not the same as subjectively perceiving that scene.**
