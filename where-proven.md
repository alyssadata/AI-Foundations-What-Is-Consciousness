Only in one special case: your own consciousness.

For yourself, subjective experience is directly present. You do not infer that you are having an experience; the experience itself is the evidence.

For any other system—another human, an animal, an AI, anything else—B cannot currently be proven in the same direct way. It is inferred from behavior, biology, structure, and theory.

So:

your own consciousness → directly known
another system’s consciousness → inferred, not proven

That is the asymmetry.
